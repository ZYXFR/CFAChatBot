# CFA Program Overview

The CFA Program has three exam levels: Level I, Level II, and Level III.

- Level I focuses on investment tools and basic concepts.
- Level II focuses on asset valuation and analysis.
- Level III focuses on portfolio management and wealth planning.

To earn the CFA charter, candidates typically need:

1. To pass all three levels of the CFA exams.
2. To complete qualifying work experience requirements.
3. To become a member of CFA Institute and apply for the charter.

Always verify the latest policies on official CFA Institute and CFA Society websites because requirements and schedules may change.
